## Gold-based and Silver-based objects

The chemical analysis coming from the ANR [**ALMACIR** project](https://iramat.github.io/almacir/) will be hosted in CHIPS.

## Copper-based objects

The upcoming DIM-PAMIR **BRONZEDATA** and ANR **ITINERIS** projects' data will be hosted in CHIPS and served as an autonomous but interoperable layers.
